Clip your Jellyfin library without leaving Discord.

Jellybot indexes subtitles, finds the quote you remember, renders an H.264 clip with ffmpeg, and posts it to your guild. Self-hosted. Docker-first. No cloud transcoding lock-in.

Try quote search Install on your server

Commands: /quote, /clip, /subcoverage. Pull the GHCR image, point it at Jellyfin, register slash commands, build the subtitle index once.

/quote

Search indexed subtitle text and clip the matching scene with padding and duration controls.

/clip

Pick a movie or TV episode, set start/end timestamps, preview privately, then post to the channel.

Self-hosted

Runs beside Jellyfin on your hardware. Health endpoint, Compose profiles, Watchtower-friendly GHCR tags.
